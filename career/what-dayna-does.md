# What Dayna does (Claude's read, 2026-10-06, for Dayna to correct)

This is built from Dayna's documents and live LinkedIn, not from word counts. It's the understanding the resume is written from. It is not copy.

## The pattern
Dayna's work keeps landing at the same point: a company changing shape. That might be a brand born online opening real stores, a heritage company modernizing its stores and its partner channel, or luxury brands shrinking through COVID. In each case Dayna builds the operating model the company needs for what comes next, across every function at once.

- **Money.** P&L, budgets and labor models (evo payroll at 14–15%, counting the labor behind online sales and labor nobody was tracking). With the CFO: margin models and 18 months of reporting fixed.
- **Physical.** Site buildouts: contracts, construction schedules, permits, tax and reporting setup, compliance. Store layouts and infrastructure at Joann. Back-of-house rebuilt for online pickup.
- **Inventory and channels.** Centralized inventory and ordering (SVP), omnichannel fulfillment through stores (evo, $7.5M), e-commerce through the stores (Collected Group), CRM and clienteling (Bonobos, SVP).
- **Rules and risk.** A safety and violence-response policy that went national (SVP), compliance on new sites, closing stores with full inventory reconciliation.
- **People system.** Job descriptions and new roles, career paths with pay attached, pay equity, cost-of-living pay, training cadence and platforms, succession (the Co-Assistant Manager model at Nordstrom), and promotions into HQ.
- **Venue.** The evo flagship was a store, a rental and service shop, a skatepark and an art gallery, and it hosted events with 500 to 1,000+ attendees. Then the campuses: Snoqualmie (mixed-use), Salt Lake City (hotel) and Seattle (property expansion).

## By company
- **Nordstrom:** learned the business from part-time sales up. Tied inventory to labor, built a succession model and a commission model, grew a department by changing the buy.
- **Bonobos:** a brand born online, testing physical showrooms. Dayna built the clienteling side (the first client book, the CRM tools) during its launch years.
- **Paisley:** first role outside retail. Took a medical practice from paper to digital.
- **BCBG:** held the top volume through brand consolidation and restructuring.
- **Collected Group:** turned around teams across three luxury brands, then closed stores and wound brands down cleanly through COVID.
- **evo:** an outdoor retailer that started online, growing into physical campuses. Dayna built its retail operating model as it went from startup to multi-site company, and ran a venue inside the flagship.
- **SVP:** heritage sewing brands. Moved 134 stores to one standard, rebuilt the field organization, and kept the Joann partnership running through its restructuring.
- **WDC / Well Lived Citizen:** the same work at small scale. Rebuilt small-business models, took a product to a patent filing, and built a business solo from the legal setup to pricing.
